## 💰 5. Revenue & Earning Maximization Strategy (Business Growth)

Aapka main goal earning karna hai, toh AdMob revenue maximize karne ke liye ye 4 key factors follow karein:

1. **Rewarded Ads are Gold (Highest eCPM $15 - $40+):**
   * Aapke game me `hint-btn` par Rewarded Video Ad integrate hai.
   * Players ko jab knot tough lagta hai, wo khushi-khushi rewarded ad dekhte hain. Isse Retention bhi nahi girti aur eCPM sabse high milti hai.
2. **Interstitial Ad Pacing:**
   * Har 1 chote level ke baad interstitial ad mat dikhayein (isse user irritate ho kar uninstall kar deta hai).
   * Best Practice: Har **2 se 3 levels** ke baad interstitial show karein, ya jab user level solve karne me 30+ seconds lagaye.
3. **Target High-eCPM Tier-1 Countries (ASO Secret):**
   * US, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany me AdMob eCPM $10 - $25 hota hai, jabki Asian markets me $0.5 - $2 hota hai.
   * Humne jo English ASO description banaya hai, wo US/UK Play Store search indexing ke hisaab se fully optimized hai taaki wahan se organic downloads aayein.
4. **Daily Challenges = High Retention:**
   * User roz app open karega toh AdMob active daily users (DAU) count badhega, jisse Google aapki ad inventory ko premium rates par bechega.
