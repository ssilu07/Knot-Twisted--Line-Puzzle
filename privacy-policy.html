Privacy Policy

Knot Twisted (com.tangledline.game)
Effective Date: April 3, 2026 • Last Updated: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Knot Twisted ("the App", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to safeguarding the privacy of our players. This Privacy Policy details the types of data that may be collected when you use our mobile application, how that information is utilized, and your choices regarding your personal data.

Knot Twisted is an interactive, free-to-play puzzle game supported by third-party advertising.

2. Information Collection and Use

We believe in privacy by design:

  • No Personal Data Collection by Us: The App does not require user registration, email sign-up, social logins, or personal profile creation. We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, phone number, contacts, or precise physical location.
  • Local Device Storage: Game progression (unlocked levels, earned stars, and audio preferences) is stored locally on your device via standard Android SharedPreferences and local storage. This data never leaves your device and is permanently removed if you clear the app cache or uninstall the application.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing game development and provide the game for free, the App displays advertisements via Google AdMob (banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded video ads).

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personal device information to deliver, measure, and optimize advertisements, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) / IDFA.
  • Technical Specifications: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and mobile network operator.
  • Coarse Location Data: General geographic information derived from your IP address (e.g., country or region level, never precise GPS location).
  • Ad Interaction Metrics: Information regarding ad impressions, clicks, views, and diagnostic crash logs.

For detailed information on how Google processes and safeguards user data, please consult:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google AdMob Data Safety Guidelines

4. Data Security & Encryption in Transit

We prioritize the security of all user interactions. All data transferred between the App and third-party advertising partners (Google Play Services / AdMob) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS protocols.

5. How to Opt-Out of Personalized Ads

You have complete control over advertising personalization on your Android device. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time:

  1. Open your Android device Settings.
  2. Select Google → All services (if applicable) → Ads.
  3. Tap Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.
  4. You can also activate Opt out of Ads Personalization to prevent tailored ad delivery.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Knot Twisted is designed for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 for residents of the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information through third-party advertising partners, please contact us at the address provided below so we can promptly address the situation.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your location, you may have legal rights regarding your information:

  • European Economic Area (GDPR): You have the right to access, rectify, restrict, or request erasure of any personal data processed by third parties, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.
  • California Residents (CCPA / CPRA): You have the right to request disclosure of categories of personal information collected, request deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data.

Because Knot Twisted does not maintain personal accounts, user profiles, or cloud databases, you can exercise these privacy rights directly by managing your device's Advertising ID settings or contacting us.

8. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

We do not operate backend servers storing user profiles or personal records. Local gameplay data is wiped immediately upon uninstalling the application.

If you have any questions or wish to submit a data inquiry or deletion request related to device identifiers handled by our ad partners, please contact us via email.

9. Device Permissions Requested

The App requests only standard, essential permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to communicate with Google AdMob servers to retrieve advertisements.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to verify active network connectivity prior to requesting ad content.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Required solely to trigger pleasant haptic feedback during interactive puzzle untangling.

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or applicable legal requirements. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage players to review this page periodically.
