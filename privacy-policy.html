Privacy Policy

Tangled Line: Knot Twisted

Effective Date: April 3, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Tangled Line: Knot Twisted ("the App", "we", "our") handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy. The App is a free puzzle game supported by advertisements.

Information We Collect

The App itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. We do not require user registration, login, or any form of personal data input.

However, the App uses third-party services that may collect certain information:

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner ads and rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect and process certain data including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • General location data (country/region level, not precise location)

For more information on how Google handles data, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps.

Data Storage

The App stores game progress (current level) locally on your device only. This data is not transmitted to any server and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The ads shown are non-personalized when possible. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data through third-party ad services, please contact us.

Permissions

The App requires the following permissions:

  • INTERNET - Required to load and display advertisements.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE - Required to check network connectivity before loading ads.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
